## F. Notes on the freeze rule

- The rule (CLAUDE.md:47-49) and the README's "living docs" line disagree, and neither covers docs/14 or docs/15. The
  concrete result: docs/12 still says "last updated 2026-10-07", docs/13 says TabView is not built, docs/14 says there
  are no tags, and docs/15 says nothing is built. Every one of those docs was meant to be living.
- decisions.md's own header (lines 3-4) forbids editing the binding tables. Superseded rows such as munit (:19),
  scalafmt (:18) and layout (:47) therefore stay wrong at the top of the most-read file, with the correction hundreds of
  lines below. Recommendation: allow a one-line `~~…~~ superseded YYYY-MM-DD (see log)` annotation in the tables. That
  keeps the audit trail without leaving a stale binding first.
